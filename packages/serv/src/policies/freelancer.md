Who this is for: a freelancer or small studio invoicing other businesses for services.

Default: Net 30, credit limit equal to the invoice, when the client is active, has traded for 3+ years, has filed recent accounts and has no gazette or court trouble.

Ask a 30% deposit when any of these is true: the company is under 2 years old; its latest accounts are 6+ months overdue; a commercial court ruled against it in the last 12 months; the invoice is above 10,000 EUR and nothing is known about its finances.

Ask a 50% deposit when: its accounts are 18+ months overdue or were never filed; or its last filed year shows a loss above 20% of revenue, or negative equity.

Prepay only when: a past insolvency proceeding ended less than 3 years ago under a continuation plan; or two or more deposit triggers fire together.

Decline when: the company is in liquidation, has ceased trading, or is sanctioned.

Conflicting signals: the most recent dated fact wins, and a hard fact (gazette, court, filing date) beats a soft one. Never offer more than 45 days.

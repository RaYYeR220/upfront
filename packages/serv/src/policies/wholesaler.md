Who this is for: a small wholesaler shipping goods to business customers on account.

Default: Net 30 with a credit limit of 20,000 EUR when the customer is active, 3+ years old and filed its accounts on time.

Net 15 and a credit limit of 3,000 EUR when the customer is 1 to 3 years old. The same when no financial figures are on file.

Ask a 30% deposit when: accounts are 6 to 18 months overdue; or a commercial court decision involved it in the last 12 months; or the order is above 20,000 EUR.

Prepay only when: accounts are 18+ months overdue or never filed; or the last filed year shows negative equity; or a past insolvency proceeding ended less than 3 years ago.

Decline when: the company is in liquidation, has ceased trading, or is sanctioned.

Conflicting signals: goods shipped cannot be taken back, so when in doubt choose the stricter of two candidate terms.

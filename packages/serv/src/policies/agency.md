Who this is for: an agency running multi-month projects with milestone invoices of 5,000 to 50,000 EUR.

Default: Net 45 with a credit limit of one milestone, when the client is active, has traded for 5+ years, filed its accounts on time and shows a profit or stable revenue in its last filed year.

Net 30 and a credit limit of half a milestone when: the company is 2 to 5 years old, or its latest accounts are 6 to 12 months overdue.

Ask a 30% deposit per milestone when: the company is under 2 years old; or revenue fell by more than 30% in the last filed year; or a commercial court ruled against it in the last 12 months.

Ask a 50% deposit when: accounts are 12+ months overdue or never filed; or the last filed year shows negative equity.

Prepay only when: a past insolvency proceeding ended less than 3 years ago; or the invoice is more than 10% of the client's last filed revenue.

Decline when: the company is in liquidation, has ceased trading, or is sanctioned.

Conflicting signals: weigh the size of the invoice against what is known; with little evidence and a large invoice, prefer a deposit.
